Kalye Life in New Times

"What you wear is how you present yourself to the world, especially today, when human contacts are so quick. Fashion is instant language." —Miuccia Prada



The best fashion store can deliver.

"Klynt" is a success made by the ambition and innovation of Mikhail Mendoza, Jamaica Bermudez, Baby Marie Dimalanta, and Lloyd Lacumba and co. to create an "artistic" representation of streetwear. With inspiration coming from different media and cultures, the clothing co. reached the goal to influence, provide, and support the youthful evolution of streetwear.

By November, the clothing co., which started as a hobby of students from TIP, with little to no knowledge in the streetwear world, decided to go with the flow of contemporary trends and culture. Each design specifically created to suit the targeted audience while allowing everyone to appreciate the design the company provides.

The brand continuously provides the masses with quality and world-standard clothing, and service for the people to enjoy. Each individual using our products is treated with comfort and style by our beloved products. We strongly believe that each product we send out to the world is made with love and care.

Our streetwear designs are inspired by urban culture, art, and youth movements. We aim to create a community around our brand, where everyone feels empowered to express their unique identity through fashion.

Additional Information


Founded by Mendoza Mikhail Pitstock in 2023, Klynt transcends mere clothing; it embodies a cultural experience. Streetwear, a fashion trend originated in streets, is known for its laid-back, comfortable, and distinctive aesthetic that gives consideration to the attitudes and lifestyle (Blogs Crew, 2023)

People express themselves in different, unique ways which includes their clothing, hairstyle, body customization (e.g., tattoos), and jewelry , and adopting what is more commonly referred to as fashion (Ross, 2020), and the fashion embodies a contemporary perspective and a set of trends to embrace. Notably , a compelling observation is that, for a significant portion of participants, fashion is predominantly associated with younger generations (Shukurova, 2018).
