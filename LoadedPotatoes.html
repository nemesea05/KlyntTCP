The best the world could offer

"Loaded Potatoes .Co" is a success born from the ambition and innovation of Khylle Mendoza, creating a flavorful and unique approach to fast food. Inspired by diverse culinary traditions and bold flavors, Loaded Potatoes was designed to bring joy and satisfaction to potato lovers everywhere.

What began as a simple idea by Khylle Mendoza in November quickly grew into a thriving concept. With a passion for creating mouthwatering loaded potato dishes, the company followed the trend of customizing fast food to deliver both quality and indulgence. Every recipe is crafted to cater to a wide range of palates while offering something for everyone to enjoy.

The brand is committed to providing exceptional, world-class fast food with a focus on customer satisfaction. Every Loaded Potatoes dish is prepared with care, ensuring that each bite delivers the perfect combination of taste, comfort, and creativity. We take pride in serving meals made with love and dedication, making every customer experience unforgettable.

Additional Information


Established in 2023, Loaded Potatoes .Co redefines fast food with its unique approach to delivering bold flavors and comfort in every bite. Known for its innovative loaded potato dishes, the brand embraces a laid-back yet indulgent dining experience that caters to diverse tastes and lifestyles.

Food has always been a way for people to express themselves, and Loaded Potatoes reflects this through its creative and customizable menu options. By blending contemporary culinary trends with timeless comfort food, the brand captures the spirit of a generation that values quality, flavor, and personalization. Whether you’re a seasoned foodie or a casual diner, Loaded Potatoes offers a modern take on fast food that truly stands out.
